Read research critically

Better questions lead to better understanding.

A compound can be interesting to study without being an established medical treatment. This library separates early experimental observations from claims supported by stronger evidence.

  • Check the model: cell experiments, animal studies and human clinical research answer different questions.
  • Check the methods: controls, reproducibility and sample size affect confidence in findings.
  • Check the material: identity, traceability and analytical testing matter in laboratory work.
  • Check the limitations: research-only availability does not establish regulatory approval for medical use.
